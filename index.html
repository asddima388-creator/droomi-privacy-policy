Droomi Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Local artwork and app data

Droomi stores drawings, coloring progress, settings, and challenge progress locally on your device. Droomi does not upload or access your artwork unless you choose to export or share it.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, purchase and entitlement status, and limited app/device diagnostics so Droomi can activate and restore Premium. Droomi does not receive your full payment-card details.

Advertising

For users without Premium who allow ads, Droomi uses Unity LevelPlay to provide rewarded ads for Pause & Draw and interstitial ads in Fire and Ice Paths. Unity and its enabled advertising partners may process IP address, device and app information, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and an advertising identifier when Apple authorizes tracking.

Droomi asks for advertising consent before initializing LevelPlay. You can withdraw that consent or exercise a U.S. sale/share opt-out from Settings > Ad Privacy Options. Denying Apple's tracking request prevents IDFA access and does not itself remove access to contextual rewarded ads.

Sharing and retention

Droomi does not sell your drawings. Advertising and subscription providers process data under their own retention and privacy practices. Your local artwork remains on your device unless you export, share, or delete it.

Your choices

  • Change advertising privacy choices in Droomi Settings.
  • Manage Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Restore purchases from the Droomi Premium screen.
  • Delete local artwork within Droomi.
